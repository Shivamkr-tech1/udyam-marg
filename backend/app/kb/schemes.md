# Prototype scheme rules

UDYAM MARG uses a 10% entrepreneur margin and 90% concessional loan structure.

Micro Finance Scheme: project cost up to ₹1.40 lakh; stated loan ceiling ₹1.25 lakh; annual interest 6.5%; 3-year tenure; 3-month moratorium.

Term Loan Scheme: project cost above ₹1.40 lakh and up to ₹50 lakh; loan up to ₹45 lakh; annual interest 8%; 7-year tenure; 6-month moratorium.

The prototype must always use the deterministic finance service for project cost, loan eligibility, scheme routing and repayment calculations. The LLM may explain those outputs but must never recompute them.
