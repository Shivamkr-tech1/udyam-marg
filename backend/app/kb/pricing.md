# Pricing guidance for demo

Pricing must be treated as indicative until live local market prices are fetched. For rural micro-enterprises, useful methods include competitor benchmarking, cost-plus checks, small-pack affordability, good-better-best tiers and testing willingness to pay with a limited pilot.

For dairy demo narratives, an illustrative standard-milk retail band of roughly ₹54–₹66 per litre may be used only as a placeholder. Production should fetch current cooperative/private dairy rates and local retailer prices.
