# Micro-enterprise SWOT guide

Strengths should be concrete local advantages such as proximity to customers, existing skills, repeat buyers, SHG relationships or transport access.

Weaknesses should include realistic constraints such as low working-capital buffer, limited inventory, lack of bookkeeping, single-person operations and limited cold/storage infrastructure.

Opportunities should be underserved customer jobs, repeat services, local institutional demand, value addition, route aggregation and digital payments.

Threats should consider seasonality, supply interruptions, price volatility, competitor response, regulation, spoilage and buyer concentration.
