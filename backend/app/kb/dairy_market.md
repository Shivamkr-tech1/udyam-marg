# Rural dairy advisory template

Potential dairy channels include doorstep subscriptions, tea shops, sweet shops, hostels/schools where procurement is feasible, milk collection centres, SHGs, weekly haats and value-added products such as curd and paneer.

Key dairy risks include animal health, fodder/feed price changes, milk spoilage, unreliable cold chain, seasonal yield variation and dependency on a single bulk buyer. Demand is usually concentrated in morning and evening windows.

A micro-enterprise should validate local raw milk procurement cost, cooperative/private dairy rates, household willingness to pay, route density, container/cold-storage requirements and daily break-even volume before scaling.
